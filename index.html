Bio

Working at the intersection of photography, ecology, and digital technology, César Augusto Ramírez (b. Iquitos, Peru) interrogates the image as a political object and as constructed reality. Shaped by his early childhood in the Peruvian Amazon, his practice interweaves concerns around the natural world, the condition of the Other, and the capacity of new technologies to fabricate alternative forms of life and alterity. His work questions the indexical nature of the image, its material limits, and the ideological forces that configure it.

Ramírez was born in Iquitos and grew up in Lima before emigrating to Paris, France in 2022. He received his Master's degree in Fine Arts from the Sorbonne (Paris 1) in 2026.

His work has been included in exhibitions Videoakt International Biennial of Art of New Media, Museum of Contemporary Art of Lima (2026); At hand and Entrez sans sonner, Galerie du Crous de Paris (2025 and 2026); Prix Michel Journiac (2025); Avatar reveal, Linda Gallery Centro de Arte (2025); ICPNA Contemporary Art Prize (2023 and 2022); Independencia: Cusco Art Biennial (2021); New Peruvian Photography, MATE Museum (2019); among others.

The artist lives and works between France and Peru.
